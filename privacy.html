Privacy Policy

"rclone personal" is a personal-use OAuth client. It is used solely by its owner, running the open-source rclone CLI tool, to access the owner's own Google Drive account.

  • No data is collected, stored, or transmitted by this application to any third party.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only locally on the owner's own devices and are used only to call the Google Drive API on the owner's behalf.
  • This application is not distributed to, or used by, any other users.
